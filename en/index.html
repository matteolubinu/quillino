Quick answers.

How much does Quillino cost?
Your first property is free forever. Quillino Pro starts at €9.99 a month or €59.99 a year, with a 7-day trial and cancellation any time, or €149.99 one time, for life and with no renewal. Pro unlocks unlimited properties, the 730 report and PDF documents, the accountant summary, year-by-year CSV export, property listing PDF, widgets and Apple Watch.
Is my data safe?
Yes. Quillino has no servers of its own: your data stays on your iPhone and, if you turn sync on, travels encrypted only to your personal iCloud. No tracking and no ads in the app. You can also lock the app with Face ID.
Does it help with my tax return?
Quillino prepares a clear annual report of income and expenses, with cedolare secca and ordinary IRPEF compared, plus a property record with cadastral data, contract registration details and rent collected month by month. All of it to hand to your accountant: it does not replace one, but it saves you both time.
Which devices does it work on?
iPhone, iPad, Mac with Apple Silicon and Apple Watch. One purchase works on all your Apple devices, with data synced through iCloud. The Android version is coming to Google Play.
Is it suitable for flats rented to students?
Yes. You can manage several tenants in the same property with per-room rents and separate payments, and enter the contract registration details for each tenant, which is handy when every room has its own contract.
Can I manage several properties and flatmates?
Yes. With Pro you manage unlimited properties, including situations with several tenants in the same property (per-room rents) and their separate payments.
What happens if Pro expires?
Your data stays where it is: Quillino deletes nothing. The first property you added stays visible; the others are hidden until you return to Pro, but their deadlines stay in the list, read-only, so you never lose one. Basic export of your data to a single file stays free.
Can I bring my rentals over from Excel?
Yes. You import an Excel or CSV file, choose which columns match property, tenant and rent, check the preview and confirm. It takes a few minutes.
Can I rely on the tax calculations?
They are an estimate: they use the tax rules of the year, and under the 730 Report you see which rules were applied and when they were last checked. They do not replace the advice of an accountant or a tax assistance centre (CAF): always check amounts and deadlines before paying or filing anything.
